# Repository Guidelines & Domain Context

Repository-specific engineering guidelines, architectural invariants, and safety constraints.

## 1. Tech Stack & Standards
- Language, toolchain, and dependency management conventions.
- Build, test, and linting procedures.

## 2. Safety & Verification Mandates
- Testing requirements (e.g. test-driven development, verification commands).
- Pre-commit and CI verification policies.

## 3. Workflow & Harness Guidelines
- Planning and specification workflows (e.g. writing specs to `docs/design/` and plans to `docs/plans/`).
- Documenting findings and learnings in `docs/` as durable documentation.
